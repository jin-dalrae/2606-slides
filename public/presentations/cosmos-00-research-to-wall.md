## Feeds turn debate into one stream

<p class="slide-ref">Act 1 · Problem</p>

Reddit, X, Discord, and forums decide order for you.

Structure exists in the thread — worldviews, bridges, gaps — but a ranked list hides it.

Loud and extreme voices rise. Readers get fatigue, not a map.

<p class="slide-closing">The problem is orientation, not only “too much content.”</p>

Note: Source: /cosmos/ intro + V1 problem framing.

---

## Offline walls already work as place

<p class="slide-ref">Act 1 · Reference</p>

Bulletin boards, poster walls, sticky notes:

- Messages accumulate over time  
- People walk, scan, step closer  
- Density and position carry meaning  
- You can read without speaking or posting  

<p class="slide-closing">That is the behavior Cosmos rebuilds — not a live party in a headset.</p>

Note: Source: /cosmos/ working thesis.

## Secondary research checked the assumptions first

<p class="slide-ref">Act 2 · Secondary</p>

| Finding | So what for Cosmos |
| --- | --- |
| Quiet reading is real participation | Reading without posting is a success path |
| Spatial audio: location helps attention | Place matters — but live voice is hard to scale |
| Async spatial social is possible | Memory and transformed presence > raw replay |
| VR reading comfort is fragile | Design for short, stationary sessions |

<p class="slide-closing">Prior art supports a wall; it does not say “build a full platform now.”</p>

Note: Source: /cosmos/secondary/ and reports 2.1–2.4.

## Primary research: four people + experts

<p class="slide-ref">Act 2 · Primary</p>

| Who | Plain finding |
| --- | --- |

## Research decision

<p class="slide-ref">Act 2 · Synthesis</p>

### Wall first. Reading before community.

1. Control seed content  
2. Prove wall browsing (comprehension, comfort, place memory)  
3. Add save / return  
4. Light contribution later  
5. Native community or spatial voice only if earlier phases work  

<p class="slide-closing">Hosting posts is not the novel claim. The wall is.</p>

## Three wavelines, one session spine

<p class="slide-ref">Act 3 · Waveline</p>

Same stages: Entice → Enter → Orient → Explore → Discover → Immerse → Interact → Exit

| Experience | Shape (plain) |
| --- | --- |
| **Feed** | High early scroll; deep read weak; exit with residue |
| **Plain VR browse** | Setup friction; body fights the UI |
| **Cosmos wall** | Orient useful; Explore calm; deep read can peak; contribute optional later |

<p class="slide-closing">Wave height = felt intensity — not “how good.”</p>

Note: Source: /cosmos/user-waveline/

## Cosmos Explore is calm on purpose

<p class="slide-ref">Act 3 · Waveline</p>

On the Cosmos wave, **Explore sits lower** than Orient or Discover.

That is a **calm scan** of clusters — not a failed climax.

Feed Explore is higher: novelty without a map.

<p class="slide-closing">Raising Explore to feed-level intensity would recreate doomscroll in a sphere.</p>

## Gaps the wall can close

<p class="slide-ref">Act 3 · Stakeholders</p>

| --- | --- |

## Weaknesses that stay

<p class="slide-ref">Act 3 · Stakeholders</p>

Even if the wall works:

- Hardware cost and comfort  
- Store fees and policy  
- Empty wall without import  
- Chat and feed still own daily habit  
- Growth capital can break patient loops  
- Reverse loyalty (people → product) not earned yet  

<p class="slide-closing">Opportunity is real. Structural one-ways do not vanish.</p>

## Impact: harms have owners

<p class="slide-ref">Act 3 · Impact</p>

| Code | Harm | Mitigate by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Premium access only | Cross-device as first-class |
| H2 | Doomscroll in a sphere | Never use dwell as north star |
| H3 | Spatial harassment / steward load | Tools before multi-user scale |
| H4 | Voice / biometric exposure | Opt-in body data; text always works |
| H5 | Empty wall | Seed + stewards before “living community” marketing |
| H8 | False impact claims | Publish falsifiers; wall-vs-feed study |

<p class="slide-closing">Impact work is incomplete until we name who can be hurt and what we change.</p>

## Proof still missing

<p class="slide-ref">Act 4 · Next</p>

Primary interviews shaped design. They are **not** the wall-vs-feed study.

Still required:

- Same seed discourse: flat feed vs Cosmos wall  
- Outcomes: comprehension, place memory, calm exit  
- Kill criterion: no advantage after novelty — or harm worse than feed  

<p class="slide-closing">Making phase 02 status: **next proof** — not “done because people liked the idea.”</p>

---

## Go / stop

<p class="slide-ref">Pressure test</p>

| Go if | Stop or narrow if |
| --- | --- |
| Wall beats feed on sense-making | No gain after controlling for novelty |
| People leave without FOMO | Product only works as dwell engine |
| Cross-device path exists | Impact only for premium headset owners |
| Seeded walls feel real | Empty spheres and wasted contributor labor |

<p class="slide-closing">If layout does not help understanding, do not add more social complexity.</p>
